Brave New World

the brave new world is, in fact, always inside

There is a very small mind sleeping in this tab.

This is not a chat. You make one wish, and this page becomes that world:
its colors, its things, its words, and the panel you are wishing from.
Then it hands you a few levers it invented. Nothing leaves your browser.

for everyone brave enough to leave the old world, whatever that means for them now
