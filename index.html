Brave New World

the brave new world is, in fact, always inside

There is a very small mind sleeping in this tab.

Wake it, and say what world you want: a place, a mood, one word, anything.
The page becomes that world, and the mind designs the panel you are wishing from:
where it sits, what the button says, and what the other buttons do.
Nothing leaves your device.

for everyone brave enough to leave the old world, whatever that means for them now
